Cyber Awareness

Cybercrime can affect anyone. Learn to recognise common online threats.

🔹 Phishing: Be careful with suspicious links and emails.
🔹 Online Fraud: Never share your OTP or banking passwords.
🔹 Identity Theft: Protect your personal information.
🔹 Social Media Safety: Use strong passwords and privacy settings.
